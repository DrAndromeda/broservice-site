Fresh Flower Delivery Across Koh Phangan

We deliver fresh flowers and hand-crafted bouquets anywhere on Koh Phangan — from Thong Sala to Haad Rin, Sri Thanu to Chaloklum. Whether you're celebrating a birthday, planning a wedding, surprising a loved one, or decorating a villa, our same-day flower delivery service covers the entire island.

Choose from classic rose bouquets, tropical arrangements with local orchids and frangipani, custom gift baskets, or wedding flower packages. Every arrangement is prepared fresh on the day of delivery.

Flower Delivery Areas on Koh Phangan

  • Thong Sala — main town, daily deliveries
  • Sri Thanu & Haad Yao — west coast, same-day
  • Haad Rin — full moon parties and events
  • Chaloklum — north coast resorts and villas
  • Baan Tai & Baan Kai — central east coast
  • Haad Salad — quiet bay area

Flowers & Arrangements We Offer

  • Rose bouquets (red, white, pink, mixed)
  • Tropical arrangements (orchids, heliconia, frangipani, palm leaves)
  • Mixed seasonal bouquets
  • Wedding flower packages (bridal bouquet, corsages, table decor)
  • Gift baskets with flowers and local treats
  • Single-stem deliveries for simple surprises

Same-Day Flower Delivery — How It Works

  1. Place your order before 2:00 PM for same-day delivery.
  2. We prepare your arrangement fresh from our floral stock.
  3. Delivery is made between 3:00 PM and 7:00 PM across Koh Phangan.
  4. You receive a confirmation photo and delivery update.

Frequently Asked Questions About Flower Delivery on Koh Phangan

How long does flower delivery take on Koh Phangan?

Same-day delivery if ordered before 2:00 PM. Delivery between 3:00 PM — 7:00 PM across the island.

Can I order flowers for delivery to a resort or hotel?

Yes, we deliver to all resorts, hotels, and private villas on Koh Phangan.

Do you deliver to Koh Samui or Koh Tao?

Yes, we can arrange inter-island delivery. Please contact us for pricing and timing.

What flowers are available on Koh Phangan?

We stock roses, orchids, frangipani, heliconia, lilies, sunflowers and seasonal tropical flowers. Special orders can be arranged with advance notice.
