Property Rentals on Koh Phangan

Find your perfect island home with BroService.in.th. We help with long-term villa and condo rentals on Koh Phangan — verified listings, fair prices, and honest advice.

Finding the perfect rental on Koh Phangan can be overwhelming with so many options. Whether you're looking for a budget studio near Thong Sala or a luxury beachfront villa with a private pool, we help you find the right place. All our listings are verified and fairly priced.

We specialize in long-term rentals for expats and digital nomads moving to Koh Phangan. Our team knows every beach and soi on the island. We help with viewings, contract negotiations, and paperwork. No agency fees — we connect you directly with property owners.
